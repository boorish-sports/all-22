## Play-by-play data — nflverse (CC BY 4.0)

The searchable index is derived from
[nflverse-data](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data): the play-by-play
release (`pbp`), the FTN charting release (`ftn_charting`, charting data
provided by [FTN Data](https://www.ftndata.com/) and published through
nflverse), and the players release (`players_components`).

nflverse-data is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
(CC BY 4.0). The derived index published at
`github.com/boorish-sports/all-22-index` is a modified form of that data: columns
were dropped, FTN charting was joined onto play-by-play, and the result was
loaded into SQLite. No endorsement by nflverse or FTN is implied.

Please credit **nflverse** (and **FTN Data** for the charting columns) if you
reuse the index or anything derived from it.
