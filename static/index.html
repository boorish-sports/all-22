The search UI lives on pro.nfl.com, not here.

NFL's clips are Widevine/FairPlay DRM played through a domain-locked THEOplayer licence, so they can only decrypt on nfl.com. The panel goes to the video rather than the other way round — one tab, NFL's own player, your own subscription.

  1. Load the extension: chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked → extension/
  2. Open pro.nfl.com/film/plays and click the gold ALL-22 tab on the right.
  3. Filter on any of the 372 pbp columns, click a play, watch it inline.

This page only serves the API: /api/search, /api/columns, /api/meta, /api/game_uuid.
