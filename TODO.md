# To do

Ideas for later, most wanted first. Several came from looking at FilmRoom
(adaptskill.training/filmroom), a paid desktop app that splits All-22 film you
supply yourself.

## Next

- **Drawing on the video (telestration).** A canvas layered over NFL's player:
  pen, arrow, circle, a laser pointer that fades, a few colours, undo and
  clear. Drawings are stored per play and per angle with the play's note, so
  they come back when the play is reopened from a cutup. Optionally pin a
  drawing to a timestamp so it shows only while paused on that frame. Never
  record or export the video: that breaks the "no video is downloaded" promise
  and very likely NFL Pro's terms.
