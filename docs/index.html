All-22 Film Search

Tip jar

A free Chrome extension that makes NFL Pro's film room searchable. Filter 337 columns of nflverse play-by-play on your own machine, click a play, and watch that clip in NFL Pro's own player.

Read this first
  • You need an active NFL Pro subscription. Without one the extension does nothing useful. Clips play in NFL Pro's own player, under your own account.
  • This is unofficial. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the NFL or NFL Pro.
  • It may stop working at any time, permanently. It relies on undocumented NFL Pro endpoints. If NFL changes them, the extension breaks, and there may be no fix.
  • No video is downloaded, copied, or re-hosted. Search is entirely local. The only requests it makes to NFL are one clip lookup per play you click, which is less than the film room itself makes.

Install

Add to Chrome Download the zip Leave a tip

Chrome Web Store is the easy route and updates itself. The zip is the fallback if the store listing is ever unavailable: unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and pick the unzipped folder. Then open pro.nfl.com/film/plays and click the gold ALL-22 tab on the right edge. The first run downloads a 15 MB play index from GitHub. After that it fetches only the weeks that changed, about 1 MB each, on its own.

What it does

Every nflverse play-by-play column is a filter: tacklers, pressures, EPA, CPOE, personnel, play action, motion, coverage, drops, throwaways. Pick a player and a role ("Will Anderson, sacks"), or build any combination of column filters, and the list of matching plays appears instantly. Click one and the Sideline and Endzone angles play in a side panel, with frame stepping, speed control, and keyboard shortcuts.

Search runs in your browser against a SQLite index built from nflverse (CC BY 4.0) with FTN charting. Nothing about what you search leaves your machine.

Privacy

The extension collects nothing and sends nothing to its author. It stores a small game-ID map and your UI preferences in Chrome's local extension storage, caches the play index in your browser, and makes requests only to GitHub (for the index) and to NFL Pro (for the clips you click, using your own session).
